# Market artwork

The mobile app uses twelve independent raster assets from three original ImageGen collections. Generated masters are retained in the project owner's local image library. The approved customer screen designs guided composition, colour and component placement. All artwork pixels listed below came from the generated masters.

| Master | Brief used for generation | Exported artwork |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `exec-eb7aaa9c-54e5-4443-9d58-df5bf6cded5b.png` · RGB · 1536 × 1024 | Four equally sized original photographs. An Islamabad restaurant exterior at dusk, a distinct Rawalpindi grill-house facade, saffron chicken dum biryani on a copper plate and chicken karahi in a cast-iron wok. Warm natural editorial lighting, architecture and food only. | `kitchen.jpg`, `second-front.jpg`, `rice.jpg`, `karahi-hero.jpg` · 768 × 512 each |
| `exec-2e5a0155-488c-4132-9fea-8ccb563d2e94.png` · RGBA · 1254 × 1254 | Four isolated tactile illustrations with upper-left light. Stacked tandoori rotis, mint raita, a hand passing stylized green Pakistani banknotes and a freestanding mobile wallet phone. | `desi.png` and `raita.png` · 627 × 600. `cash.png` · 700 × 645. `wallet.png` · 546 × 645. |
| `exec-33ecf04e-f93e-427b-92af-c77ba6d9b00a.png` · RGBA · 1254 × 1254 | Four individual Pakistani servings on transparency. Chicken biryani in a brass handi, charcoal chicken tikka skewers, tomato-and-ginger chicken karahi, and a chicken paratha wrap. Consistent three-quarter-overhead view and warm miniature styling. | `biryani.png`, `bbq.png`, `karahi.png`, `wrap.png` · 627 × 627 each |

Each export selects its illustration or photograph at its generated pixel size. Alpha is preserved for the illustrations. Restaurant and dish photography depicts illustrative venues and servings. The live catalogue supplies names, prices and availability.
